How to Order

Step by step guide:

NOTE: Kape Tankulan is only open on weekdays from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

To place an order, follow these steps:

  1. Visit our menu page.
  2. Select the items you would like to order.
  3. Proceed to the checkout page.
  4. Enter your delivery information.
  5. Review your order and submit.
